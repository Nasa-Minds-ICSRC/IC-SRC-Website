Who Are We?

Our club organizes research teams focused on developing robotic technologies to support space exploration for NASA competitions. We're always open to collaborating with other people to expand research opportunities across all fields!

Why Should You Join?

  • Apply academic skills to real-world projects
  • Collaborate with other people excited about changing the world
  • Learn about internship and scholarship opportunities through club collaborations and past alumni club members

READY TO LAUNCH YOUR CAREER?

Open to all students across all majors. No experience required. Just bring curiosity and drive.

How to Meet with Us

The club lab is in room 361 of the Biological Sciences Building at Cal State LA. We have weekly club meetings every Friday from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM. On Fridays, the lab is kept open for everyone to work in from 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

When Are Team Meetings?

  • Cryo-UAV Team: Friday, 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM
  • Cryo-Bot Team: Friday, 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM
  • Cryo-Sense Team: Friday, 1:30 PM – 3:00 PM

NASA MINDS SE-Paper
Cal State LA Cryo-UAV Team

Our Cryo-UAV team took first place in the Systems Engineering paper category at the NASA MINDS competition, a national program connecting university students with real NASA engineering challenges.

ALUMNI SUCCESS

David San

2020 Alumni · CS
"As a CS major, it was eye-opening to work alongside mechanical engineers and hear firsthand what Dr. Hays brought back from his time at JPL. I even got to pass that knowledge down to newer members as the club grew. Now I work at JPL myself, mostly doing backend development, APIs, and a little of everything else. The club was a real stepping stone."

Sophia Sherzai

2025 Alumni · EE/BME
"The club was my first time applying engineering in a real group setting outside of the classroom. It ignited my passion for the field, taught me how to work with a team, and opened doors I hadn't expected. Dr. Hays was supportive well beyond just the competition. After NASA MINDS, opportunities started opening up. Now I'm at Harvard MGH doing neurotechnology research on epilepsy and neural stimulation."

David Camacho

2021 Alumni · CS
"Unlike other group projects I was part of, ICSRC gave me real opportunities to contribute and help others grow. I built skills in leadership, systems engineering, and programming robots with Python that I still use today. The influence of Dr. Hays and my teammates is hard to put into words. I would not be where I am today without NASA MINDS. Now I work as an associate software engineer with this project still on my resume and portfolio."
