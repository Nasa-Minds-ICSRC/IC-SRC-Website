Who Are We?

Our club organizes research teams focused on developing robotic technologies to support space exploration for NASA competitions. We're always open to collaborating with other people to expand research opportunities across all fields!

Why Should You Join?

Whether you're in CS, engineering, physics, or any other field, there's a project for you.

  • Build and test real hardware (drones, hexapods, and rovers) in our own dedicated lab
  • Develop practical skills through club workshops in soldering, 3D printing, and Python programming for drones and other hardware
  • Connect with industry professionals at Boeing and JPL, and access club resources and funding to develop your own research project ideas

READY TO LAUNCH YOUR CAREER?

Open to all students across all majors. No experience required. Just bring curiosity and drive.

OUR ADVISOR

Professor Charles C. Hays

Dr. Hays is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Cal State LA. Before joining CSULA in 2013, he spent 11 years as a Senior Member of the Technical Staff at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), where he contributed to the Genesis, Planck, and Mars Science Laboratory missions. He received a NASA Group Achievement Award for his work on the MSL ChemCam instrument team.

His research spans Condensed Matter Physics, Materials Science, and Instruments for Space Science. His most recent publication is "Background Studies for the MINER Coherent Neutrino Scattering Reactor Experiment."
